Privacy Policy for Posture

Last Updated: December 25, 2024

Introduction

Posture is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Locally Stored Data

All settings are stored locally on your device:

  • Notification preferences
  • Reminder times and frequency
  • Onboarding status

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall.

Analytics (Anonymous)

We use Firebase Analytics:

  • Screen views
  • App opens
  • Device type (anonymous)

We do NOT collect: Name, email, location, photos, or health data.

Crash Reports

We use Sentry to fix bugs:

  • Error logs
  • Device type

Data Storage

Local data is protected by iOS security. Third-party data is processed by Firebase and Sentry (GDPR compliant).

Notifications

Local notifications are scheduled on your device. Disable anytime in Settings.

Medical Disclaimer

Important: This app provides general guidance only and is not medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional for chronic pain or health concerns.

Your Rights

  • Delete: Uninstall to remove all data
