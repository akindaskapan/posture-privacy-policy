# Privacy Policy for Posture **Last updated: December 2024** **Developer: Akın Daşkapan** ## Introduction Posture ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application. ## Information We Collect **We do not collect any personal information.** Posture is designed with privacy in mind: * All your settings (notification times, frequency preferences) are stored **locally on your device only** * We do not operate any servers that receive your data * We do not collect analytics or usage data * We do not track your location * We do not access your contacts, photos, or any other personal data ## Data Storage All app data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms provided by the operating system. This includes: * Notification preferences * Reminder start and end times * Reminder frequency settings This data never leaves your device and is deleted automatically when you uninstall the app. ## Data Security Since all data is stored locally on your device, we rely on the security features provided by iOS (such as device passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, and sandboxed storage). We do not transmit, share, or process your data on external servers. ## Third-Party Services Posture does not use any third-party services that collect or process personal information, including: * No analytics services * No advertising networks * No crash reporting services * No social media SDKs ## Notifications The app uses **local notifications** to remind you about posture. These notifications are: * Scheduled entirely on your device * Not sent through external servers * Fully controllable by you (can be disabled anytime in device settings) ## Children's Privacy We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the app does not collect data at all, there is no risk of child data exposure. ## GDPR & CCPA Compliance Because Posture does **not collect, store, process, or transmit any personal data**, GDPR and CCPA rights (access, deletion, correction, data portability, etc.) do not apply. Users may delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the application. ## Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
